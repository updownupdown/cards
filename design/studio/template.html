The spade mark

Three that start from the original interlacing ribbons and move towards Nocturne, three variations on the current contour mark, and four new directions. Each shows large, as the app icon, and beside the name as it sits on Home.

Three alternates for every icon

The 68 icons the app uses (24 more were never referenced, and have been removed). Each alternate belongs to a family, so picking one family throughout keeps the set consistent; pick per icon where one reads better.

Card backs

One back for every game, in the theme's colours

Games no longer have their own colours, so these share one back across the app and take the colours of the chosen theme: change the theme above and every back follows. The last two use each game's own artwork instead (shown here with two games' art).
