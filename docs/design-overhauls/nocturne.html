Direction A

Nocturne

The Night Gallery's language, grown into the whole app. One idea runs through it: what you've earned is lit. Won games hang in colour under a picture light, unwon ones wait in the dark, and every progress bar is a strip of lamps.

  • One accent. Lamp gold is the only colour the UI owns. Everything else comes from the art. The reds, blues, greens and purples of today's buttons go.
  • Two typefaces. Instrument Serif italic for anything with a name (games, works, authors, chapters, quotes); Outfit, the app's current font, for controls and numbers.
  • Four tabs, renamed. Tonight · Voyage · Gallery · Search. Collect becomes Gallery: one room per category, no stamps. A lit room is a completed category.
  • Reading rooms. The Tao and the quotes open from the top of Voyage. Both are drawn in lamp light: constellations in gold thread, portraits as a single neon-like line.
  • The table is dark, with the game's own colour as a warm lamp from above and its art faintly in the weave. Cards are ivory; backs are navy with a brass lattice.
  • Winning switches the painting's light on. The quote follows, then the buttons.

Risk: low. It's the closest to today's app and could be rolled out gradually: tokens first, then screens.
