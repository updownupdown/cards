Direction C

Prism

The chrome steps back and the artwork becomes the interface. There's no house accent colour: every screen takes its tint and highlight from the painting in front of you (each game already has gameColorDark and gameColorPale), so the app looks different with every game but always hangs together.

  • Modern and quiet. Manrope, set heavy and tight, for the interface. Fraunces light italic only for words that aren't ours: quotes, the Tao and art credits. White pill buttons, glass surfaces.
  • Three tabs: Now · Odyssey · Library. Collect and Search merge into Library: the whole catalogue is the gallery, in colour once won and greyscale until then, grouped into rooms by category. There's nothing separate to collect.
  • Odyssey holds the map, the Tao and the voices behind one segmented control. On the map, games are planets painted with their own art.
  • The table is the painting, blurred and dimmed. The card backs are the art too, so each game has its own deck without drawing any new backs.
  • Winning un-blurs the painting into full view. That's the reward moment, and the quote card rises over it.
  • Big, bold card indices for readability; the hint glows in the game's own colour.

Risk: medium to high. Merging tabs changes habits, and the adaptive colour needs a contrast check per artwork (the pale colours all look safe on dark).
