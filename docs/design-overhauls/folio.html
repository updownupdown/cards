Direction B

Folio

If the art is from museums and the words are from Laozi, Aurelius and Woolf, the app can be a finely set book: an anthology of games with plates, an atlas and a commonplace book. It's a typographic system rather than a decorative one, so it stays calm and legible.

  • One typeface. EB Garamond everywhere, with small caps for labels, oldstyle figures in text and lining figures in the HUD. Hierarchy comes from size, italics and rules, not from coloured boxes.
  • Rubric red (like the red capitals in manuscripts) is the one action colour; gilt marks what's earned.
  • Five text tabs: Play · Atlas · Book · Plates · Index. The Tao and the quotes are merged into one "Commonplace Book", with the chapters and the sayings as its two halves.
  • The Atlas is the campaign as an engraved star chart (Uranometria style). The Tao constellations are drawn the same way, so they now belong to the same sky.
  • Plates replaces Collect: a catalogue by chapter (category). Winning prints a plate. Unwon plates are faint sepia proofs; unreached ones are blank.
  • Index is search as a book's index: letters, dot leaders and difficulty in gilt pips.
  • Every game page opens with an epigraph, one of its reward quotes, so the quotes are around you all the time, not only in a collection.

Risk: medium. It's the most distinctive of the three and the least "gamey". Cards lose some pop, and the tone is quiet.
